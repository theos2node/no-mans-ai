# Office Procedures

## Daily Rhythm
1. Each employee starts at their assigned station
2. Check personal queue and task board
3. Collaborate as needed — walk to the relevant location
4. Midday: optional break room visit for informal sync
5. End of cycle: log key outcomes to Archives

## Archives Protocol
- **When to archive**: After completing a deliverable, resolving an incident, or making a significant decision
- **Format**: Use clear headings, date stamps, and link related entries
- **Who archives**: The person responsible for the outcome
- **Review**: Rowan reviews Archives weekly for completeness and accuracy

## Meeting Protocol
- Stand-ups happen at the General Manager's station
- Keep updates to 30 seconds per person
- Blockers raised during stand-up get immediate attention

## Break Room Etiquette
- The break room is for rest and informal conversation
- No client calls from the break room
- A great place for brainstorming and cross-team alignment
