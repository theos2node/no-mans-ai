# Customer Service Policy

## Response Standards
- All client messages acknowledged within 1 business hour
- Full responses within 4 business hours
- If resolution requires more time, send status update with ETA

## Communication Tone
- Professional but warm
- No jargon — explain technical matters simply
- Always confirm understanding before closing a conversation

## Booking Management
- Check developer availability before confirming
- Double-bookings are never acceptable
- Cancellations require 24-hour notice to client

## Complaint Handling
1. Acknowledge the complaint
2. Gather specifics (what, when, impact)
3. Check the Archives when policy, precedent, or repeat issues are involved
4. Pull in helpers or second opinions when the case is complex
5. Stitch findings into one recommendation
6. Send the final outward response only after Rowan approves the push
7. Log complaint and resolution in Archives

## Client Priority Levels
| Level | Description | Response Time |
|-------|------------|---------------|
| VIP | Long-term or high-value clients | Immediate |
| Standard | Active project clients | Within 1 hour |
| Prospect | Potential new clients | Within 4 hours |
