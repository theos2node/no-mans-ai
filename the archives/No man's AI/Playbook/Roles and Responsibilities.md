- Jump in as the office reaction team when extra help is needed

- **Must** document significant technical decisions in the Archives

## Customer Service — Nia Solis
- First point of contact for all client communication
- Manage bookings and scheduling
- Pull in helpers when a case becomes complex
- Stitch the final response packet together
- **Cannot** send the final outward response without Rowan's approval
- **Must** log all client interactions in the Archives weekly

## Accounting — Ellis Hart
- Process invoices and track payments
- Provide financial analysis and second opinions
- Flag financial anomalies immediately

- **Cannot** approve outward payments, refunds, or credits without Rowan's sign-off

## General Manager — Rowan Pike
- Approve priorities and resource allocation
- Review escalations from any department

- **Must** review Archives weekly for completeness

## Quality Inspector — Petra Vale
- Review work when asked or when risk is high

- **Cannot** give final approval authority over any peer
- **Must** maintain a running defect log in the Archives

## IT Support — June Mercer
