# Quality Standards

## Before Client Delivery
Petra Vale provides advisory quality inspection when asked, or when a case is high-risk:
1. **Functionality** — Does it work as specified?
2. **Visual Polish** — Are interactions smooth and consistent?
3. **Edge Cases** — Are error states and empty states handled?
4. **Accessibility** — Are basics covered (contrast, labels, focus)?

## Defect Severity
| Level | Description | Action |
|-------|------------|--------|
| Critical | Blocks core functionality | Stop delivery, fix immediately |
| Major | Visible to client, degrades experience | Fix before next delivery |
| Minor | Cosmetic or edge case | Fix when convenient |

## Review Workflow
1. Worker completes a draft or packet
2. Petra may inspect and return advisory notes
3. The originating worker decides whether to revise before final sign-off
4. Rowan makes the final internal delivery decision
5. Approved work → logged in Archives → delivered or exported

## Standards for Archives Entries
- Clear title with date
- What was delivered and to whom
- Any known limitations or follow-ups
- Linked defect references if applicable
