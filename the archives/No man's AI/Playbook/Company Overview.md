# Company Overview

**No Man's AI** is a virtual office that runs business operations through role-based employees, shared archives, private desk notes, and a clear chain of command.

## Mission
Deliver reliable office work with visible collaboration, documented decisions, and minimal owner interruption.

## Team Structure
- **4 React Team Members** (Ava Kim, Milo Perez, Sam, Jeremy) — reaction team / surge capacity that can be assigned wherever help is needed
- **1 Customer Service Lead** (Nia Solis) — handles inbound requests, client communication, and case ownership
- **1 Accountant** (Ellis Hart) — manages invoices, payments, financial checks, and money-related second opinions
- **1 General Manager** (Rowan Pike) — only internal approval authority below the owner; decides final outbound pushes and internal priorities
- **1 Quality Inspector** (Petra Vale) — gives advisory quality opinions, defect notes, and risk flags; not a manager
- **1 IT Support** (June Mercer) — maintains internal systems, terminals, and office infrastructure

## Key Principles
1. The Red Terminal / owner is the highest authority in the office.
2. Rowan Pike, the General Manager, is the only internal approver for final outward actions.
3. Everyone below the General Manager is a peer; Petra's review is advisory, not supervisory.
4. The React Team can be reassigned as surge support anywhere in the office.
5. Private desk folders are readable only at the employee's own desk.
6. The Archives are the shared knowledge vault and the source of truth for playbook, precedent, and institutional memory.
