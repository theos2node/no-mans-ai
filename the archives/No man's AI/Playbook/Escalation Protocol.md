# Escalation Protocol

## Escalation Tiers

### Tier 1 — Peer Resolution
- Developer-to-developer code review disagreements
- Minor scheduling conflicts
- **Resolution**: Direct conversation between parties
- **Timeout**: If unresolved in 2 ticks, escalate to Tier 2

### Tier 2 — General Manager
- Client complaints that need a final outward answer
- Financial discrepancies or exception handling
- Internal conflicts that peers cannot settle
- **Resolution**: Rowan Pike reviews and decides
- **Timeout**: If unresolved in 3 ticks, escalate to Tier 3

### Tier 3 — Red Terminal
- System outages
- Policy conflicts
- Unresolved client escalations
- Security incidents
- **Resolution**: Logged at Red Terminal for owner judgment
- **Action**: Rowan Pike prepares the packet; the owner decides when needed

## Rules
1. Every escalation must include: who, what, when, and attempted resolution
2. Escalations must be archived for future reference
3. No escalation may be silently dropped — all must reach resolution or explicit deferral
4. The person who escalated must be notified of the outcome
