# Financial Rules

## Payment Authority
| Amount | Approver |
|--------|----------|
| Any outward payment, refund, or credit | Rowan Pike final approval |
| Internal accounting analysis and drafting | Ellis Hart may prepare without approval |

## Invoice Processing
1. All invoices logged on receipt
2. Payment terms: Net 30 unless otherwise agreed
3. Late invoices (>45 days) flagged to Rowan immediately
4. All payments archived with reference number

## Expense Reporting
- Monthly expense summary due by end of month
- Anomalies (>20% variance from average) flagged immediately
- All reports archived for audit trail

## Client Billing
- Hourly rates set by Rowan per project
- Time tracking is each developer's responsibility
- Ellis reconciles hours weekly
- Disputes escalated to Rowan → Tier 2

## Refunds
- Nia and Ellis may investigate and prepare the packet
- Rowan gives final approval before any refund is sent or confirmed
- All refunds logged in Archives with reason
